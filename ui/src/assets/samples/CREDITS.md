# Sample photographs

The Image Control preview's samples are genuine RICOH GR III JPEGs, straight from the camera
(EXIF Software "RICOH GR III Ver. 1.41", unedited, unstitched) and shot with Image Control
**Standard** (maker note ImageTone 0x100), the rendering GR Mod's films are applied to. They are
from Wikimedia Commons, by Stephan van Helden, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: scaled from 6000 x 4000 to 2400 pixels
on the long side (the portrait one turned upright as its EXIF orientation says), JPEG quality 93.
gr3-r0000022.jpg and gr3-r0000023.jpg (Sacré-Cœur, Montmartre) are the project author's own GR III
Standard JPEGs, given for these previews, scaled the same way. None of the samples is covered by the
licence of GR Mod's code.
