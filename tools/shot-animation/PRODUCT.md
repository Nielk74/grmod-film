# Shutter studies

This local browser lab develops a small, satisfying post-shot animation for a Ricoh GR III screen. The primary audience is the camera owner reviewing character, motion, sound and readability before firmware integration.

## Confirmed direction

- The selected mascot is the third CRT character, Diskette Terminal: charcoal case, crooked antenna, expressive green face, ivory hands, orange sneakers and a tiny photograph.
- Retain all three approved transitions: Disk Dash, Pixel Portal and Rocket Delivery. Pick randomly from shuffled sets of three, with no immediate repeats. Manual Play controls select a specific sequence for inspection.
- Each sequence has a 1.4-second entrance, a full five-second acting routine and a 1.2-second exit. Entrances and exits use distinct illustrated action poses, anticipation, travel and follow-through. The robot faces its direction of travel and turns before changing direction.
- Keep the approved Pocket Lab sound at 100% application volume, plus synchronized effects for entrances and exits. Muting, cancellation and browser audio failures must work.
- The latest requested visual adjustment is a more neon palette throughout the character and its effects, while retaining its approved artwork, personality and choreography. Keep the dark case and black ink; accent the face with acid lime, sneakers with fluorescent orange and photo with magenta/violet/blue. Avoid added glow or rendering passes.
- Keep the sprite approach inexpensive: twelve cached 128 × 128 poses, 30 fps sampled transforms and a bounded set of geometric effects. High-resolution art is for the review page; camera-size previews use the native sprite dimensions.
- The user requested the globally installed pbakaus/Impeccable agents for the whole design experience, including art, color, motion, presentation, layout and final review.

## Surface and success

The browser lab is an Experience surface with inspection controls. Its three-column desktop comparison and stacked mobile view show each sequence at a large review size and in a simulated camera LCD. The owner can trigger a random shot, inspect a specific option, pause, scrub, replay either transition and mute sound. The page clears the chosen overlay at 7.6 seconds and honors reduced motion.

This is a refinement of the existing approved world: warm neutral review page, Barlow Condensed display type, dark ink, restrained vermilion interface accents, and original raster character art. A palette study is reference material, not a replacement drawing or an approved page comp. No fresh visual-world concept round is needed for this adjustment.

## Integration truth

The camera target is the exact official Ricoh GR III 2.10 image. The native implementation now covers the approved current animation, owned overlay, UI timers, transitions, camera mute and owned sound through exact-byte and original-ARM execution checks. Its offline packager produces an experimental hardware-test candidate. It preserves the official version and photo-processing code and accepts no previously modified input.

The trigger is a completed native still-image backend recipe; synthetic execution verifies the terminal notification in thirteen native plans, including nine without a preview operation. One notification per physical shutter press or saved file in every drive/file mode, physical display and audio, actual frame performance, and DMA/cache behavior still require camera tests. Browser/emulator checks do not establish physical-camera operation. Building this candidate does not flash the camera or publish a release.
